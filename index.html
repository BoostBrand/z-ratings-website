FAQ

Common questions before a demo.

What data do you need to get started?+

Standard sales, inventory, and purchasing exports from your POS or ERP. We normalize and clean the data — you don't need it perfectly formatted.

How fast do we see results?+

Most clients receive their first full set of brand ratings and SKU actions within days of connecting data — and weekly thereafter.

Is this only for large retailers?+

No. Single stores use the Core Platform to buy smarter, while multi-store operators use Silver and Gold for budget allocation and analyst oversight.

Can I try a single brand review first?+

Yes — the one-off Brand Analysis gives you a pre-purchase assessment, product guidance, and a suggested budget without a subscription.
