The process

Four steps to confident buying.

  1. 01 01

    Connect your data

    Share standard sales, inventory, and purchasing exports from your POS or ERP — a one-time setup. No integrations to build and no perfectly formatted spreadsheets required; messy real-world data is exactly what we expect.

    Takes ~15 minutes · works with most POS/ERP systems

  2. 02 02

    We normalize & score

    Our engine cleans and standardizes the data, then scores every brand and SKU on the financial factors that actually move cash. The result is one consistent rating standard across your whole catalog — and every location.

    First full set of ratings within days

  3. 03 03

    Get ratings, budgets & actions

    You receive brand and SKU grades, a recommended weekly inventory credit per brand, and a clear action on every line item — expand, hold, discount, or liquidate. Decisions arrive ready to use, not buried in a dashboard.

    Grades · budgets · expand / hold / discount / liquidate

  4. 04 04

    Act, recover & repeat

    Buy with confidence, clear aging stock, and free up trapped cash. Ratings refresh every week as new data flows in, so your decisions stay current — and improve as the picture sharpens.

    Refreshed weekly · ongoing

What's in a rating

The four factors behind every grade.

Each brand and SKU is evaluated on the financial signals that determine whether inventory protects cash flow or quietly erodes it.

Sell-through velocity

How quickly units move relative to stock on hand — the clearest signal of real demand.

Emphasis · high

Margin contribution

The real profit each brand adds — not just revenue — after discounts and carrying cost.

Emphasis · high

Discount dependency

How much a brand relies on markdowns to sell — a hidden drag on margin if it's high.

Emphasis · medium

Aging & dead-stock risk

How much capital is trapped in stock that's slowing down — and how soon it's at risk.

Emphasis · medium

The rating scale

One grade tells you what to do.

Every brand and SKU lands on a simple A–F scale, and each grade maps to a clear, recommended action.

AExpand

Strong performer. Back it heavily and protect shelf space.

BMaintain

Solid and steady. Keep stocking at current levels and monitor.

CWatch / discount

Slowing down. Trim orders and use targeted discounts to move it.

D–FLimit / liquidate

Eroding cash. Stop reordering and liquidate to recover capital.

A weekly rhythm

Your first full set of ratings lands within days of connecting data. After that, scores refresh weekly so every purchasing decision reflects the latest performance — no manual rebuilds.

Your data stays yours

Sales and inventory data is handled privately and encrypted. We use it to generate your ratings — never to share or expose your numbers.

FAQ

How it works, in detail.

How long does setup take?+

Connecting your data is about a 15-minute, one-time task. Your first full set of brand and SKU ratings typically arrives within a few days.

Do I need clean, formatted data?+

No. Standard exports are fine — our engine handles the normalization and cleanup so you don't have to.

How often do ratings update?+

Weekly. As new sales and inventory data flows in, brand and SKU grades, budgets, and actions refresh automatically.

What exactly do I receive?+

Brand and SKU letter grades, a recommended weekly inventory credit per brand, and a clear action on every item. See the solutions and packages pages for the full scope.
