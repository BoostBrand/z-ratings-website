Who we are

Inventory is a financial decision. We make it one you can defend.

Z Ratings is an inventory risk intelligence company built for cannabis retail. We turn the messy reality of brand and SKU performance into clear ratings, budgets, and actions — so every purchasing decision has a number behind it.

Why we exist

Retail was buying on instinct. We thought it deserved evidence.

Cannabis retailers face thousands of SKUs, volatile demand, and capital tied up in stock that may never move. Yet most purchasing still happens on gut feel and scattered spreadsheets — and the cost shows up later as dead stock and trapped cash.

We built Z Ratings to change that. By scoring every brand and SKU on the metrics that actually move cash, we give buyers, operators, and finance teams one defensible standard for where every inventory dollar goes.
