Pricing FAQ

Questions about packages.

How is pricing determined?+

Pricing is tailored to your number of stores and catalog size. Book a demo and we'll scope the right package and quote it for you.

Can I upgrade or downgrade later?+

Yes. Most clients start with Bronze or Silver and move up as their operation grows. You can change packages at any billing cycle.

What's the difference between a subscription and Brand Analysis?+

Bronze, Silver, and Gold are ongoing subscriptions that rate your whole catalog continuously. Brand Analysis is a one-off pre-purchase review of a single brand — ideal before committing to a new vendor.

Is there a contract or lock-in?+

No long-term lock-in. Subscriptions are flexible and you can cancel anytime — onboarding is always included.
