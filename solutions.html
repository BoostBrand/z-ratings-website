Solutions FAQ

Common questions.

Which solution should I start with?+

Most retailers start with Brand Ratings and SKU Risk Analysis to fix purchasing decisions, then add Cash Recovery and Inventory Budgets as they scale. A demo helps us point you to the right starting place.

Do these solutions work across multiple stores?+

Yes. Multi-store operators use the same ratings standard to allocate budget and compare performance consistently across every location.

What POS or ERP systems do you support?+

We work from standard sales, inventory, and purchasing exports, so most POS and ERP systems are supported. We handle the data cleanup on our end.

How do solutions map to packages?+

Core ratings, SKU risk, budgets, and discount analysis are in every plan; market intelligence and analyst oversight scale up with Silver and Gold. See the packages page for the full breakdown.
